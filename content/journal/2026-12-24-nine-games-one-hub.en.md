At the end of the year it seems worth walking through the whole shelf. play.ipe.rest now lists nine games. They are not nine versions of one idea. They are nine ways of using a short break.

## Strategy against an AI

Iltonggi is a quick territory-conquest game set in Korea's Three Kingdoms era, turn-based, with a map hidden in fog. Inkisle sends fleets between islands to tint the sea your color. Patchland takes land three ways, by tracing, placing and spreading, and keeps your wins on one map. Claimball is a duel on a notebook grid where the tile your ball lands on becomes your land. Sunmeld is a merge duel in one shared tub.

## Puzzles and toys

Dusk Blocks is a block puzzle we want to feel calm. Bottled Marbles sorts colored marbles into bottles. Tottertop is a one-tap tower of blocks that sways, aiming at rounds of about a minute.

## Something that waits

Moss Garden is a garden that keeps growing while you are away.

## What they share, and what they do not

All nine are meant to be started and stopped without ceremony. Beyond that, they differ, including in money. Tottertop is free with no ads and no in-app purchases. Some are free to try with a single purchase, and the rest are described on their own cards. We wrote about that in more detail earlier this month, and the cards carry what is settled so far.

## Where they stand

When we wrote this in mid-November, every card on [play.ipe.rest](https://play.ipe.rest) said either "coming soon" or "in development", and none of the nine was released. That may have changed by the time you read this, so the cards are the place to check, not this page.
