A lot of puzzle games add pressure because pressure is easy to design. Dusk Blocks is meant to feel calm: we are aiming for a game that does not push you to hurry.

## How it is meant to play

You have an 8 by 8 board and three pieces at a time. You place them so that full rows and columns clear, and then three new pieces arrive. When none of the pieces can fit, the round ends.

## No need to hurry

A break is already short, so a game that pushes you to hurry asks you to rush through your own rest. Here, we want a round to be a quick thought or a long look at the board, and neither to be wrong.

## Placeable, as far as we can make it

A puzzle you cannot win is just a way to feel bad. So the aim is to deal sets of three that can be placed on the board as it stands. A round can still end, but we want it to end because of your choices and not because the game dealt you something impossible.
