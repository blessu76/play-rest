Few things are as quietly satisfying as putting things in order. Bottled Marbles turns that into a game: colored marbles are mixed across glass bottles, and you move them until every bottle holds a single color.

## How it works

You pick up the top run of one color and pour it into another bottle, as long as the color matches and there is room. The level is won when each bottle holds a single color. The exact rules are still being worked out.

## Why sorting can rest you

We think the pleasure is in the order getting better a little at a time. Each move is small and easy to read, and the board looks calmer after it than before. There is nothing to react to. It is thinking, but the slow kind, with a result you can see.

## Making a level fair

A sorting puzzle can turn nasty when a mistake early on quietly makes the level impossible. We want two answers: a way to take back a bad pour, so a mistake costs you little, and a check on each level before it reaches you, so that levels nobody can solve do not get through. Both are plans, not finished features.

## Where it stands

Bottled Marbles is planned as a free game with optional rewarded ads. It is marked "coming soon" on [play.ipe.rest](https://play.ipe.rest) and is not released. It is still being built, the rules above may change, and its name could still change.
