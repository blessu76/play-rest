Few things are as quietly satisfying as putting things in order. Bottled Marbles turns that into a game: colored marbles are mixed across glass bottles, and you move them until every bottle holds a single color.

## How it works

Each bottle holds four marbles. You pick up the top run of one color and pour it into another bottle, as long as the color matches and there is room. Every level has a couple of empty bottles to work with, and it is won when each bottle is full of one color or empty. Later levels bring more colors.

## Why sorting can rest you

We think the pleasure is in the order getting better a little at a time. Each move is small and easy to read, and the board looks calmer after it than before. There is nothing to react to. It is thinking, but the slow kind, with a result you can see.

## Making a level fair

A sorting puzzle can turn nasty when a mistake early on quietly makes the level impossible. We have two answers. You can undo as many moves as you like, so a bad pour costs you nothing. And each level is checked by a solver before it reaches you, which throws out levels that cannot be solved and ones that are too easy.
