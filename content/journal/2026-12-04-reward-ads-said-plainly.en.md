Today it is about money, because the answer is not the same for every game, and one sentence would hide that.

## The one fully free game

Tottertop is free. It has no ads and no in-app purchases, and its card says exactly that.

## Which games charge, and how

Claimball and Sunmeld are free to try, with one purchase that unlocks everything, as their cards say. Moss Garden plans in-app purchases, and the price is not set. Inkisle's pricing is not set before release. Dusk Blocks and Bottled Marbles have no price on their cards yet. Iltonggi and Patchland are described on their own cards.

## What we hold ourselves to

Whatever a game's money model is, it should not depend on a trick. A price should be easy to find before you start, and a game should still be worth a break without a purchase.

## What we do not know yet

None of these games is released. Prices and in-app items have not been decided, and we will not quote numbers until they are. We will write about them plainly when there is something to say.
