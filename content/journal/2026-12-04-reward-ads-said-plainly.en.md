Today it is about money, because the answer is not the same for every game, and a single sentence would hide that.

## What a rewarded ad is

A rewarded ad is one you choose to watch. Nothing interrupts you. If the game offers something, such as one more try after a loss, you can say yes and watch a short ad to get it, or say no and carry on. It is never a wall between you and the next round.

## Which games plan them

Four of our newer games are planned to be free with optional rewarded ads: Tottertop, Moss Garden, Dusk Blocks and Bottled Marbles. The ideas we have so far are small. Tottertop and Dusk Blocks offer a continue after a lost round, Moss Garden offers a boost to what the garden earned while you were away, and Bottled Marbles offers an extra empty bottle on a hard level. These are plans, not a finished feature list, and they can change before release.

Not every game works this way. Iltonggi, Claimball, Sunmeld and Patchland have no ads on their cards, and Inkisle plans none either. Claimball and Sunmeld are free to try with a single purchase that unlocks everything, and Patchland is a paid game with no ads.

So "no ads" is true of some of our games and false of others. We would rather give you the list than a slogan.

## What we hold ourselves to

Whatever a game's money model is, it should not depend on a trick. An optional ad should stay optional, a price should be easy to find before you start, and a game should still be worth a break without either.

## What we do not know yet

None of these games is released. How often an ad is offered, and what exactly it offers, has not been tested with real players. We will write about it plainly when there is something to say.
