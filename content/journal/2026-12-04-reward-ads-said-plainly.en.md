Today it is about money, because the answer is not the same for every game, and a single sentence would hide that.

## What a rewarded ad is

A rewarded ad is one you choose to watch. Nothing interrupts you. If the game offers something, you can say yes and watch a short ad to get it, or say no and carry on. It is never a wall between you and the next round.

## Which games plan them

Four of our newer games are planned to be free with optional rewarded ads: Tottertop, Moss Garden, Dusk Blocks and Bottled Marbles. The ads are optional: you choose whether to watch one. What an ad would offer in each game is still being worked out. These are plans, not a finished feature list, and they can change before release.

Not every game works this way. Iltonggi, Claimball, Sunmeld and Patchland have no ads on their cards, and Inkisle plans none either. Claimball and Sunmeld are free to try with a single purchase that unlocks everything, and Patchland's card says no ads and no subscription.

So "no ads" is true of some of our games and false of others. We would rather give you the list than a slogan.

## What we hold ourselves to

Whatever a game's money model is, it should not depend on a trick. An optional ad should stay optional, a price should be easy to find before you start, and a game should still be worth a break without either.

## What we do not know yet

None of these games is released. Where an ad would appear, how often, and what exactly it would offer have not been decided or tested with real players. We will write about it plainly when there is something to say.
