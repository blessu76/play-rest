Moss Garden is a garden you plant, water and then leave. While you are away, the garden keeps growing, and when you come back it tells you what happened.

## What you do

The plan is that you plant, tap to water, and let the garden grow. That is the shape of an idle game, and we want it to be a very quiet one. Other features are still being worked out.

## What "while you are away" should mean

An idle game is one place where a break-sized idea can go wrong. If the game punishes you for leaving, it becomes a chore. If it sends reminders, it becomes a nag. If it grows without limit, the numbers stop meaning anything.

So we have three wishes for the garden. Leaving should never cost you anything you had. The garden should wait, not call you. And what it earns while you are gone should be settled in one calm moment when you return, with a limit, so the growth stays something you can read.

## An honest word about money

Moss Garden's card says in-app purchases, with the price not set. We will not write a price until it is decided.
