**In short:** this website collects basic visit statistics through Google Analytics, remembers your language and theme in your own browser, and asks for no name, email or account. It shows no ads and does not sell data.

## 1. Information we collect

play.ipe.rest ("the Site") is a portal for games operated by an individual developer, {{OPERATOR}}. The Site has no accounts or sign-in, and we do not ask for your name, email or contact details. We collect basic visit statistics through Google Analytics 4, as described in section 2.

The Site shows no advertising.

## 2. The website

### Visit statistics (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 to see which pages are read. It records page views, the page you came from, your browser and device type, an approximate location (country or city) and clicks on links that leave our site (the link address only). It sets cookies in your browser (such as `_ga`, which can last up to two years) to tell visits apart. We do not ask for your name or email in analytics, and we cannot identify you from it. Google processes this data on our behalf, which can include servers outside Korea.

### Stored in your browser

When you pick a language or a theme, we save that choice in your browser's local storage (under the names `play-lang` and `play-theme`). It stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can clear it in your browser settings. Fonts are served from the site itself.

### Email you send us

If you write to [{{EMAIL}}](mailto:{{EMAIL}}), we receive your email address and whatever you include. We use it only to reply and to handle your request, and we keep it as long as needed for that. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

### Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any host, it receives technical request data such as your IP address; GitHub's own privacy statement applies to that.

## 3. How we use it

To understand what is read and improve the site, to reply to your messages, and to keep the site working and secure. We do not use it for profiling, and we do not sell it.

## 4. Third parties and the games

Google (analytics) and GitHub (hosting) process data as described above. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires. The Site links to each game's own official website. Each game has its own privacy policy, and what a game collects is described there, not here.

## 5. Your choices

- Block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's [Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). The site works the same without them.
- Clear site data to remove your saved language and theme.

## 6. Questions and your rights

To request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal information, or to ask any question, write to [{{EMAIL}}](mailto:{{EMAIL}}). We reply within 10 days. Analytics data is not tied to your name or email, so we usually cannot find an individual in it; email correspondence we can locate and delete.

## 7. Children

The Site is not directed to children under 14 (or the minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
