We aim to reply within a few business days.

## For a game-specific problem

Each game has its own official site and its own support. Please start there, from the game's card on the [home page](/#games).

## Helpful details to include

- The page you were on, or the game you mean
- Your device and browser
- What happened, with a screenshot if you have one

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I play any of the games yet?

Not yet. None of the four games is released, and none has a store page. The status of each game is on its card on the [home page](/#games).

### Do you collect personal information?

No. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for details.

### Where are the terms of use?

This portal has no terms of its own. Each game's terms are on its own site, linked in the footer.
